## Judge rubric (PERSONA, in order — first differing criterion decides)
1. Genuinely useless, worst/pointless, in one line. A real/useful/merely weak power, or
   sprawling past one line, loses. A curse, or a power that quietly helps, is NOT useless.
2. Uselessness lands as a joke: reader pictures the power in use and laughs at how
   little it achieves.
3. Unexpected: nobody has heard it before (NOT slow flight / invisible-while-unwatched).
4. Tight and specific, concrete detail fixing the power in the world, not vague abstraction.
5. Clear on a single reading, no setup beyond the sentence.
